About

How I got here

My work sits at the point where public health data meets a decision someone actually has to make. That habit started early in my career and has carried through everything since.

I came up through field epidemiology at the Florida Department of Health, spending my days on case investigations and contact tracing during the COVID-19 response, before moving into clinical trial data management at Emory's Vaccine and Treatment Evaluation Unit. Since 2022, I have carried that same instinct into real-world evidence and data science work at CDC's Division of Healthcare Quality Promotion: the conviction that a dataset only matters once it reaches someone who has to make a decision with it, whether that is a nursing home clinician, a CMS analyst, or a member of CDC leadership. That is the thread running through the epidemiology, RWE, biostatistics, and data science lenses on this site. They are different job titles for the same underlying habit: get the data right, then make sure it is understood..
